PooHead

A 1996 DOS card game, restored from the original Pascal source

Before playing: tap a face-up secondary card to bring it into your hand, then tap a primary card to fill an empty face-up slot. With all three slots filled, play from your primary hand to start. Tap or left click a card to play it. Hold for half a second or right click cards of the same value to select several, then tap or left click one of them to play the selection. Tap or click the pile to pick it up. Scroll arrows are at the sides of your hand; the tiny square in the upper left opens the card counts.
